## Investigation notes

These reports document individual alerts and activity chains investigated in the room. Assessments reflect only the evidence available for each event. A suspicious classification is not the same as a confirmed incident, and a confirmed phishing attempt does not by itself prove successful compromise.

The room remains in progress. Additional alerts will be added as separate reports when their evidence has been reviewed and the findings are sufficiently supported.
